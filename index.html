Chapter Two

A letter, page by page

My love. I've started this letter a hundred times in my head and every time I get stuck at the same place: how do you tell someone they're the whole sky? Language keeps offering me small words and I keep refusing them because they don't fit you. So I'll just say it plainly: you are the best thing that has ever happened to me, and I don't know what I did to deserve it.

— 1 of 5 —

Before you, I thought love was a big loud thing — grand gestures, loud music, grand declarations. Then you came along and love became a quiet text in the middle of the day. A hand on my back when I'm tired. A laugh I can hear through a wall. You made love small enough to fit inside ordinary life, and somehow that made it bigger than anything I'd imagined.

— 2 of 5 —

I love the way you care about things. I love that you can be so soft and so fierce in the same minute. I love that you say what you mean and mean what you say — even when it's inconvenient, even when it's about me. You're honest in a way that makes me want to be honest too. Being loved by you makes me braver than I was before you.

— 3 of 5 —

I know I'm not always easy. I know I get things wrong. But I want you to know that on every single day — even the ones where we don't quite meet in the middle — I am still choosing you. Still turning toward you. Still thinking, "her. always her." That hasn't changed once. I don't think it ever will.

— 4 of 5 —

Thank you for being born. Thank you for letting me witness you. Thank you for being the kind of person I get to brag about. I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope it gives you everything you've been quietly hoping for. And I hope I get to be right there, holding your hand, when it does. 💫

— 5 of 5 —
